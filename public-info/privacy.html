Subway Runner privacy information

Subway Runner is operated by the developer reachable at abirahomod12113@gmail.com. This notice describes the consolidated Android game and its online services.

Guest play and local data

You can play without signing in. Scores, progress, selected runners and settings are stored on your device. We ask for an age group to control advertising features; we do not request a date of birth. The game does not initialize or request ads for children, teens or players who have not selected an age group.

Play Games sign-in and cloud progress

If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games and Firebase Authentication authenticate your game account. The game may display your player name. The backend uses a Firebase player identifier and hashed account identifiers to associate your saved progress, unlocked runners, inventory, Diamond balance, runs and transactions with your account. Firebase identity tokens are held in the Android host and sent over HTTPS to the game service; they are not given to the game webpage.

Cloud records are stored in the developer's Google Cloud Firestore project. Cloudflare Workers processes authenticated requests. Offline play cannot guarantee that recent progress has synchronized; the game displays connection and synchronization status.

Purchases and rewards

Google Play processes Diamond purchases. The developer's service verifies purchase tokens, maintains balances and prevents duplicate grants. We do not receive your card number or bank credentials. Purchase tokens awaiting finalization are encrypted in server storage. Daily limits use server time in UTC, rather than the device clock.

Optional rewarded advertising

Only players who select the adult age group may explicitly request a rewarded ad. There are no automatic ads, banners, interstitials or app-open ads in this Android application. Google AdMob and its consent platform process ad and consent information, which may include IP address, device information and ad interaction events. Advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed from this app; that does not mean the ad provider processes no device information. Required regional consent is requested before ads are loaded. Ad privacy choices are available in Settings.

For rewards, AdMob sends a signed server callback containing an account identifier and an opaque reward-intent identifier. The service verifies this callback before granting a reward. Closing an ad or receiving a client callback alone does not grant a reward. Development/internal builds use Google's test ad units until live testing and configuration are complete.

Use, security and retention

Data is used to operate the game, synchronize progress, fulfill purchases and rewards, enforce daily limits, provide support and prevent replay or duplicate transactions. The app does not include a separate analytics SDK. Google, Firebase, Cloudflare and Google Play process information as service providers under their own terms and privacy practices.

The server denies direct client access to Firestore records. Authentication, TLS, transaction checks and restricted server credentials protect online operations. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Account data is retained while the account is active or needed for pending operations. Minimal hashed transaction/deletion markers may remain after deletion for duplicate prevention, refunds and legal obligations. These are not an active player profile. Google Play and advertising providers retain their own records under their policies.

Delete an account or request assistance

In Settings, choose Delete my account, sign in again and confirm. The game reports success only after the server confirms deletion of its player records and Firebase Authentication account. If purchase finalization or a provider request fails, deletion remains pending and is retried; the interface must not report it complete prematurely. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games profile, cancel Google records, or automatically issue a refund.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling the app or contact support. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details.
