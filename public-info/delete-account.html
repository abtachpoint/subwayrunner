Delete your Subway Runner account

In the game

Open Settings → Delete my account. Sign in again with the linked Play Games account and confirm deletion. The server deletes the game's cloud profile, Diamond wallet, inventory, progress and Firebase Authentication account. Success is shown only after the server confirms completion. Pending requests are retried when provider/purchase-finalization work has not completed.

Without the app

You can request deletion by emailing the developer. Include the game name, your player name and, if available, the player/account identifier shown in the game. Support must verify ownership before deleting data; merely knowing another player's name is not authorization.

Request deletion by email

This email route is a manual support request, not an automatic account deletion. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, private keys or payment-card details. If the email button does not open a mail app, write to abirahomod12113@gmail.com.

What remains

Minimal hashed transaction and deletion markers may remain for duplicate prevention, refund handling and legal obligations. Google Play, Google Play Games and advertising providers retain their own records. This request does not delete your Google account, remove its Play Games profile or automatically refund purchases. Local guest progress remains on the device until app data is cleared.
