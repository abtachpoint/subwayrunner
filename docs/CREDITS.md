# Credits and licences

## City geometry (CC0)
The neighbourhood slices in `web/assets/city_extra.js` are composed from models in Kenney's
**City Kit (Commercial)** and **City Kit (Suburban)** (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
CC0 needs no attribution; credit is given here voluntarily. The licence texts are kept in
`tools/third_party/kenney/`. The palette textures are baked into vertex colours by `tools/meshkit.py`.

## Everything else
The human runners, foot-over bridge, street lamps, UI, sounds and music are generated by code in this repository
(`tools/build_human_runners.py`, `tools/build_city_assets.py`, `web/audio.js`, `web/ui3d.css`).

## Optional font
`web/assets/fonts/README.txt` explains how to add Lilita One (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
Nothing is bundled by default.

## Google Play Games mark
The sign-in button uses a neutral gamepad icon drawn in `web/ui3d.css`, not Google's official logo. If you want the
official mark, use the asset and rules from Google's Play Games brand guidelines (it is a trademark) and replace the
`.pg-logo` background image.
